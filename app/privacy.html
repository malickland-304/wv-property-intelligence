Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Who we are

This website (malickland.net) is operated by Phil Malick, a West Virginia real estate salesperson (license WV0029577) with WV Real Estate Agency, LLC, Sheila Judy, Broker, 501 E Main St, Romney, WV 26757. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean MalickLand and Phil Malick.

What we collect

  • Information you give us in our forms and chat: your name, email address, phone number, your message, and what you are looking for (for example property type, location, timeline and financing).
  • Your contact preferences, including whether you agreed to be contacted by phone or text.
  • How you found us: the page you arrived on, the referring website, and campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source), saved with your inquiry.
  • Basic technical data our server needs to run and protect the site, such as request logs and your IP address for rate limiting. We do not sell this data or use it to build advertising profiles.

How we use it

  • To answer your inquiry and help you buy, sell or research property.
  • To keep a record of your inquiry so we can follow up, and as required for real estate brokerage record-keeping.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business:

  • Our brokerage, WV Real Estate Agency, LLC, which supervises our real estate activity.
  • Service providers that process data for us: website hosting, email delivery, Google Workspace (email and records), and AI tools that help draft replies, which a person reviews before anything is sent.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.

Text messaging: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your consent to receive texts and your phone number are never sold or shared with anyone for their marketing.

Cookies

The public pages of this site do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Cookies are used only for the private admin area. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records for as long as we need them to help you and to meet brokerage record-keeping requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts. Ask us to stop calling or emailing and we will.
  • Email phil@malickland.net to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. We may keep what the law requires us to keep.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact
